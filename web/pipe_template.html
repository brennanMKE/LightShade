Green Pipe Shade

A classic platformer-style green pipe that drops over the light base. Every wall, lip included, is a single 1.2 mm layer, so the whole pipe glows evenly. The open top lets light spill up onto the ceiling.

Printing

  • Print upside down, with the rim on the bed. Use pipe_shade_print.stl or the 3MF, which are already flipped. No supports: inside the lip the bore widens through a 45° cone. Add a brim, because only the 1.2 mm rim touches the bed.
  • Use translucent or light green PLA. Opaque green will block most of the light.
