Scooter Night Shade

A 6-inch cylinder for the 82.19 mm light ring. Scooters, helmets, a wheel and scattered dots are cut into the inside of a thin 1.8 mm wall. The shapes glow brightest through a 0.8 mm skin, and the rest of the wall still glows softly.

Printing

  • Print upright with a brim, and no supports. The pockets are only 1 mm deep, so each ceiling overhangs by 1 mm at most.
  • Use white or natural PLA with a 0.4 mm nozzle. The 0.8 mm skin is exactly two perimeters.
  • The wrap is stretched around the 87.6 mm shade, wider than the 3-inch cardstock tube it was drawn for. Shapes come out about 13% wider than drawn.
