Printing

  • Print upright with no supports. Each pixel's ceiling is a 2.3 mm bridge.
  • Use white or natural PLA with a 0.4 mm nozzle. The 0.8 mm skin is two perimeters, and the 0.85 mm webs between pixel columns print as two clean lines.
  • The wrap is stretched around the 90 mm shade, wider than the 3-inch cardstock tube it was drawn for. Pixels come out about 18% wider than tall.
