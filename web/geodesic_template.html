Printing

  • Print upright, collar on the bed.
  • The upper cap is a shallow overhang on the inside. Enable tree supports, touching the build plate only. They come out through the 84 mm opening.
  • White or natural PLA/PETG. Use 0.4 mm nozzle and 0.2 mm layers, so each panel is exactly 2 perimeters.
